\title{The CAMS reanalysis follows column dust in time but not surface PM$_{10}$ across the Abu Dhabi
monitoring network}

\begin{abstract}
Global reanalyses that assimilate aerosol optical depth are used to estimate surface
coarse particulate matter (\pmten{}) where monitors are few. We evaluate the CAMS reanalysis
against the 19-monitor regulatory network of the Emirate of Abu Dhabi, October 2022 to May 2024,
and identify dust independently with a sun photometer and airport weather reports. First, the \pmten{} values that the OpenAQ archive holds for this
network are 24-h running means, not hourly concentrations. Compared with instantaneous model
values they understate the correlation (0.37 instead of 0.50) and produce an apparent 28--34\%
error reduction by statistical correction that is no longer established when model and
observations are averaged alike. Second, the reanalysis places dust in time but not its surface concentration: its dust
optical depth follows the AERONET coarse-mode optical depth (Spearman 0.85), yet its deficit in
the regional \pmten{} level grows from 14 to 32~\ugm{} from the least to the most dusty third of
days. Over the conurbation a local deficit, present at all seven metropolitan monitors on
the least dusty and on clear days, is unrelated to modelled wind speed and dust.
Of the between-monitor variance of mean \pmten{}, 46\% lies inside single $0.75^{\circ}$ cells.
The regional daily level is reproduced (Spearman 0.67), but mean bias ranges from $-128$ to
$+40$~\ugm{}, and exceedance of the WHO 24-h guideline is poorly detected
(equitable threat score 0.09). Archived averaging intervals should be checked before model evaluation, and
reanalysis \pmten{} does not represent a particular place in an arid city.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}
\item Archived PM$_{10}$ were 24-h running means, which created an apparent correction gain
\item CAMS dust optical depth tracked AERONET (Spearman 0.85) but surface PM$_{10}$ fell short
\item CAMS total AOD matched AERONET, but dust took too small a share of the column
\item A local deficit over the city persisted on clear, low-dust days at all 7 monitors
\item Up to half of the between-monitor PM$_{10}$ variance lay inside single model cells
\end{highlights}
